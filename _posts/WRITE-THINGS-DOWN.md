Write things down.

With the rise of LLMs and the general rise of frantic context switching its tempting to always go, go, go, go. Forcing yourself to slow down and write down what you've done, read or need to do increases the friction for frantically grabbing the next shiny object. Instead you engage yourself with activities that align with your life tenets.

Writing is a form of spaced repetition which is the ultimate learning device known to man kind. Working through a tough problem and then wrangling it into words further solidifies that knowledge.

It serves as a knowledge base for yourself and others.

The writing doesn't need to be perfect, grammatically correct or structurally perfect. LLMs with their multitude of downsides still tend to function much better than `ctrl + f`.
